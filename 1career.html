Career Aspirations

My long-term career goal is to become a Data Analyst working within the public health or technology sector. This role appeals to me because it combines my passion for problem-solving, data interpretation, and improving decision-making through evidence. I am particularly interested in how data analytics can be applied to healthcare outcomes, public services, and user experience design.

Through my BSc (Hons) Data Science course at Nottingham Trent University, I've gained exposure to data analysis techniques, statistical modelling, and real-world applications. Attending industry guest lectures and workshops — including sessions from NHS Digital, Capgemini, and Experian — has helped shape this goal by highlighting the practical, high-impact work data analysts do.

I have also actively participated in:

  • Volunteering as a community tutor, where I analysed student progress and engagement.
  • Extra Python workshops focused on Pandas and Seaborn for data visualization.
  • Contributing to a GitHub-based data project analysing regional health statistics.
These experiences demonstrate both my enthusiasm and suitability for a career in data analytics.

Reference: Prospects - Data Analyst Job Profile

Required Skills and Knowledge

Becoming a data analyst requires a combination of technical, analytical, and professional skills. Based on research from job sites (e.g. Indeed, Prospects, and LinkedIn) and feedback from industry professionals during lectures, the following skills are crucial:

Skill Type Required Skill Source / Rationale
Technical Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib) Course + LinkedIn Jobs
Technical SQL and database querying Indeed + NHS Data Analyst jobs
Technical Data visualization (Power BI, Tableau) Experian guest lecture
Analytical Interpreting data trends and anomalies Capgemini workshop
Communication Presenting insights to stakeholders Prospects, workshops
Project Management Handling datasets within deadlines Personal projects
Ethics & GDPR Responsible data handling Industry lectures, university ethics module

Conclusion

My career goal to become a data analyst is based on substantial research and supported by a variety of experiences including coursework, external lectures, and volunteering. I’ve identified both strengths and areas for growth using multiple reliable sources, and I am actively pursuing development opportunities to close skill gaps. This alignment between aspirations, research, and self-assessment reflects a strong and well-evidenced commitment to achieving my career goals.
